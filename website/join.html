Join

Get an account on the demo box yourself, at demo.dregg.online/join. No one needs to add you by hand.

Everything on this box is world-readable. Every member can read the whole demo Store, and your Mini signing key is held on the box (a hosted account): readable by the operator, by your own account and by no other member's. Keep nothing private or precious there.

Reading needs no account: the documents members publish are at demo.dregg.online, a static snapshot refreshed every few minutes. Writing happens in the hosted shell over SSH.

  1. Make an SSH key if you have none: ssh-keygen -t ed25519.
  2. At /join, choose a name and paste the public half (~/.ssh/id_ed25519.pub). Keep the status page it gives you: its address is your request's only key.
  3. Choose free (the box funds a small starting balance while it lasts) or pay (50 DREGG a week on Solana; your wallet, SSH key and account are then publicly linked on-chain).
  4. When the page says your account is ready, ssh -t mini-s-NAME@demo.dregg.online opens the mini> prompt. The guide is FRIENDS.md.
  5. To use Mini from Discord, tick "link Discord" and type the code the page shows: /mini line:link CODE.
  6. To publish a document to the public snapshot, delegate observe on it to the public reader (delegate ID DOC PUBLIC observe 0, submit ID, publish ID); revoke takes it down at the next refresh.

What to expect

The demo is redeployed every few days and may be re-genesised: your SSH key stays, and everything you made is erased. FRIENDS.md lists what the shipped node has.

Contributing

Read the README, AGENTS.md and the developer guide. Nothing in Mini has users, so a format change is a rebuild: an old shape must refuse to load, and the commit says what it breaks.

Every claim here maps to a file at main (SOURCES.md). The repository overrides this site.
