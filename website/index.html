Mini

Mini is one shared world in which people, applications and agents submit writes to the same Lean-owned admission path. An admitted write yields a durable world root; a refusal is explained by the clause that refused it; an attempt whose reply was lost stays undecided and keeps its exact retry, so nothing is done twice.

Documents, authority, money, programs and applications share that one path. A computation can propose a change without acquiring permission to make it: computation proposes, authority admits.

Read this first. This is a temporary public demo. It shuts down on 11 November 2026, and everything on it is erased then; it may also be re-genesised before that. Everything on the box is world-readable, and a hosted account's signing key is held on the box (readable by the operator and by your own account, no other member's). Privacy is not audited. "Machine-checked" here names the admission path's theorems, not every deployed behaviour. Keep nothing private or precious there.

Try it

  • The application at demo.dregg.online: what members chose to publish, readable without an account.
  • Join at demo.dregg.online/join: a name, a hosted key and one link to your session. The free route has a fixed number of seats (the door's default is 50) and closes earlier if the sponsor's balance falls below its floor. The paid route costs 50 DREGG a week on Solana, and your wallet, ssh key and account are then publicly linked on-chain.
  • What this edition carries: what you will be able to do when the door opens, what is still being built, and what is not in this edition.
  • nano's testground, the sibling rebuild on the same box.

The opening target is 29 October 2026. Until the door opens, the application and the join page may answer 503 (a closed door), and pay-to-enrol is not yet live. Status by component says what is live, each line with the run it rests on.
