# The lamplighter's note
There is a house at the end of the lane where a lamp upstairs has hummed for forty years, low and patient.
Nobody remembers who lit it. The lamplighter left a note on the hallway table: "The lamp wants a key, and the key wants a door."
The door is north of the hallway, behind the anteroom, and it opens for anyone the story's law allows.
You can walk the house yourself in the story room called tale. Type: story list   then   look
It is a small tale with a sealed law: you can only go where the law lets you, and the law can be read before you try it.
