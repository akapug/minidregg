# The lost reply
Networks drop answers. If you ask Mini to do something and the reply never reaches you, you cannot know whether it happened.
Most systems let you ask again and hope. Mini keeps your exact attempt, undecided, until you look it up.
The retry is not a second attempt. It is the same attempt submitted again, and the world sees one effect and one charge, however many times you press it.
Try it: type "history" and look for an attempt marked undecided, then type: retry   with its name, or "lookup" to ask what became of it.
The idea is in what-is-mini. The refusal side of the story is in a-refusal-explains-itself.
