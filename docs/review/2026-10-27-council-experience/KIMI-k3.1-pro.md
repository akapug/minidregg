## A. Is the thesis right?

The defensible thesis is narrower and stronger: **one Lean-owned admission path judges writes from people, applications and agents against explicit capabilities, member-installed laws, read roots and funding** (`README.md:27-34`; `docs/review/2026-10-26-deploy/AFFORDANCES.md:445-470`). The striking demo is not a receipt stream; it is an ordinary collaborative action crossing those boundaries and producing a useful judgement.

Two phrases overclaim:

- Receipts contain `acceptedCount` and `worldRoot`; explanation comes from separate Host views and retained attempts (`docs/review/2026-10-26-deploy/AFFORDANCES.md:468-469`; `native/resource-client/src/shell.rs:68-69`). Acts do not automatically leave “receipts that explain themselves.”
- “Nothing else a stranger has used does that” is an unverified comparative. Remove it rather than defend it.

Also, “machine-checked rules” must not imply theorem coverage of every deployed behaviour: C1 still names the refusing-path gap and moves receive-path `World.admit` gating to Phase 3 (`docs/DEMO-PREFIX-2026-10-26.md:210-212`).

The stranger-stopping sequence is: **install a law that refuses even its owner; watch the same law stop an agent; lose a reply and retry exactly without a second effect**. The first and last are already identified as demonstrable affordances (`docs/review/2026-10-26-deploy/AFFORDANCES.md:594-600`). That is more distinctive than publishing the box’s own log.

## C. The parity law (§1a)

As written, derivation from the verb table is a **mirage**. `Verb` contains only `name`, `usage` and `operation`, all strings (`native/resource-client/src/shell.rs:48-52`). The `doc` row alone packs dozens of alternatives, JSON inputs, files and flags into `usage` (`:90`). `subcommands` literally scrapes words from that prose (`:115-133`). There is no typed argument, effect or result schema from which reliable forms can be emitted.

L12’s actual mechanism—calling `shell::line` and rendering `(exit, text)`—can produce functional form wrappers, but it does not establish visual parity (`origin/lane/web-frontend:docs/WEB-FRONTEND-2026-10-27.md:77-84`).

The correct derived artifact is a **structured command registry** shared by shell and web: subcommands, typed fields, reference kinds, uploads, dangerous confirmations and outcome schemas. Generic forms are the default face, not the whole product.

The smallest honest check would:

1. Enumerate every top-level command and subcommand, including chat, Hermes and story.
2. Require a registered non-terminal route and a renderable no-JavaScript form for each.
3. Red-proof itself by removing one route and naming it.
4. Separately execute each tour flow and assert its decoded outcome.

Until every subcommand has an execution/outcome test, call that result **face coverage**, not behavioral parity.

Bespoke faces are justified for documents/transclusion/history, grants and law editing, `can`/`why`, rooms and residents with purse meters, receipts and exact retry, applications, and the stateful story/market/job verbs.

## D. “The box is a member” (§2)

**Sound as a same-admission demonstration; theatre as independent assurance.** A signed turn proves that `mini-s-box` was authorized to append a claim. It does not prove that a ship, restore or certifier drill actually happened: the reporter, key and system being reported on share one trust domain.

Give `mini-s-box` an append-only capability to the operations topic and no administrative reach. Publish an allowlisted event schema: event kind, candidate hash, check outcome, timestamp and safe failure category.

Potential leaks include exploitable details in raw red notices, operational timing, member or agent identities in fleet receipts, and archive correlation through backup metadata. A digest must never be presented as meaningful evidence of restore correctness by itself.

The public room must never contain keys, tokens, provider credentials or prompts, session identifiers, cookies, CSRF values, member documents or private rosters, raw environment/configuration, command lines, core dumps, operator-socket output, or unredacted failure logs. Keep detailed security incidents in a restricted operational record; publish only their safe classification and remediation state.

## E. The ambitions A0–A9

Ranked by tour value per day:

1. **A1 — browser-only join:** small and prerequisite to everything.
2. **A0 — visual parity:** benefits every step, after replacing the registry mechanism.
3. **A2 — agent under law:** the strongest cross-boundary demonstration.
4. **A4 — `/tour`:** turns surfaces into a timed product and acceptance artifact.
5. **A5 — real applications:** familiar collaboration makes unfamiliar authority legible.
6. **A7 — story/market rows:** small additions; story already has a concrete law demonstration.
7. **A8 — member-defined kinds:** deep programmability, but larger and currently ownerless.
8. **A3 — operations room:** useful supporting transparency, not a centrepiece.
9. **A6 — oracle grain.**
10. **A9 — second node.**

**Drop A6 and A9 from the fifteen-minute tour and its critical path**, without deleting either surface. Their present cost buys less understanding than the core authority demonstrations.

Add **A10: the lost-reply button**. Let a stranger deliberately lose a response, inspect the undecided attempt, retry exactly, and verify one effect. That is the missing “I have never used anything like this” moment. Fold a guided “change this law and repeat the attempt” interaction into A8.

## F. Three sentence replacements

1. Original:

   > “Mini is **one shared world in which people, applications and agents act under one set of machine-checked rules, and every act leaves a receipt that explains itself.**”

   Replacement:

   > “Mini is one shared world in which people, applications and agents submit writes to the same Lean-owned admission path; admitted writes yield durable world roots, refusals can be explained by clause, and undecided attempts retain their exact retry.”

2. Original:

   > “The shell already holds each verb's arguments and help text; the web body renders a form from that table (argument → field, reference arguments → pickers over `refs`, outcomes → the one outcome partial), and only the places that deserve a bespoke view (a document page, a board, a room, a receipt, the grants graph) get one.”

   Replacement:

   > “A structured command registry holds each verb’s subcommands, typed arguments, reference kinds and outcome schema; shell and web both derive from it, generic forms are the default face, and workflows with real visual structure receive bespoke views.”

3. Original:

   > “A stranger who doubts the system reads its own operations log as receipts in the same world they are standing in.”

   Replacement:

   > “The operations room shows operational claims admitted under Mini’s own laws; because the box signs those claims itself, they disclose what the box reported, not independent evidence that the reported event occurred.”
