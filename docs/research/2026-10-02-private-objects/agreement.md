# Agreement scope before an agreement algorithm

Research lane D, 2026-10-02. **READ** denotes inspected source/paper sections; **DERIVED** is analysis; **PROPOSED** is an unproved design. This is research only: no builds, tests, services, source mutations or new runtime qualification. Preferred hypothesis: retain one ordered atomic admission domain initially, refine its semantic dependency contract, and admit independent domains only after proving their observable equivalence. Escrow is a narrower, useful special case. None of these choices requires importing a full consensus DAG.

## Three requirements that should not be collapsed

**DERIVED:** logical commutation means swapping two permitted operations preserves enabledness, logical state, each operation's permitted reply, authority-dependent disclosures and emitted effects. Equal final object values are insufficient: an increment returning a balance, a time-sensitive grant, an output-release decision or a shared quota can distinguish swaps. Two exact-preimage writes to different fields of the **same whole-root target** are not currently independent commands.

Physical journal equality is stronger and different. Different legal linear extensions produce different heights and chain digests; clock/ambient-height observations can then change computation. Either preserve current globally ordered height semantics or deliberately define a quotient observation contract and per-domain progress/frontiers. Calling equal application state “same history” hides a real compatibility change to receipts, certification, replay and admission.

Dependency privacy is independent again. Public object IDs, authority IDs, predecessor hashes, conflict flags, retry patterns and touched-domain sets expose linkage even when values are sealed. A dependency commitment hides neither repeated equality nor graph size automatically. An independence classifier must be both **sound** and disclose only what its declared audience may learn. Conservative classification is safe but can stall progress and expose coarse shared-group membership.

## Alternatives and discriminating partition trace

Let X and Y have independent state and grants g and h. I invokes X under g; J writes Y under h; R revokes g. Start from X=0, Y=0 and valid g/h. Partition the authorization/order authority from a replica serving I. On the other side R commits, its client receives a durable acceptance, and only afterward I is first submitted on the isolated side. **PROPOSED contract:** after that completed revocation, a fresh I cannot receive irrevocable semantic success. An earlier admitted I remains replayable as history; revocation does not erase its receipt. Local preparation may continue, but current-right acceptance needs a fresh fence/quorum or must wait. J can progress independently only if its full dependency and persistence scopes really exclude g.

A. **One ordered domain with selective dependency guards — preferred first hypothesis.** Keep common atomic admission/replay/certification; classify semantic reads more finely to avoid unnecessary invalidation and to prepare later concurrency. R before I refuses I; I before R accepts I and then R. Concurrent overlap has one chosen order. J can be prepared concurrently, but in today's receiver loses a racing head CAS and must be re-admitted on the new head. Removing the whole-authority guard requires validated per-grant/key/policy projections and omission proofs; it does not itself yield partition availability. Cost: shared sequencer/finality availability and global receipt order. Benefit: smallest semantic change, intact multi-target atomicity, existing durable identity and exact readback.

B. **Independent object/domain execution plus explicit conflicts.** Give X/g and Y/h independent admission/certification domains; include policy heads, delegation dependencies, clock, replay namespace and charges in each actual conflict closure. J completes in Y during X's partition. R and I must still share a fence or agreement scope even though one is an authority update and the other a content write. Causal delivery alone does not order concurrent R/I. If R has already completed before I begins, a stale isolated X must refuse/block I under the stated contract. If X uses a previously delegated lease instead, acceptance through lease expiry is an explicit weaker revocation contract. Cross-domain transfers require distributed atomic commitment or a specified asynchronous workflow; two local successes are insufficient. Cost: reconfiguration, cross-domain transactions, recovery and receipt/frontier changes, plus conflict graph privacy.

C. **Escrow partitioning for bounded resources.** Replace a shared quota with separately owned spend rights, allocated by an ordered authority transition. With global quota 10 split 6/4, each partition spends at most its local allocation; it cannot locally borrow the other's unused rights. That improves availability for the quota operation, not for arbitrary exact-preimage updates or policy changes. R cannot instantly cancel already delegated spend rights at an unreachable site while also permitting irrevocable disconnected spending: choose a revocation fence, lease expiry with a trustworthy clock, or a documented outstanding-right liability. Rights reassignment after crash must prevent resurrection/double ownership. Byzantine sites additionally need non-equivocating spend evidence and enforced conservation; signatures on invented counter state do not supply it.

For the unrelated-write subtrace, prepare J at authority A, then change only g, producing A′. A whole-authority-root settlement guard invalidates J even if h and every Y authorization predicate are unchanged. That is a **conservative implementation conflict**. In contrast, changing g invalidates I semantically. Advancing the clock is conservative for a law proved time-insensitive; it is semantic for expiration/time predicates. Any classifier that ignores a current law's ability to inspect those slots is unsound. Authority and clock broad guards are therefore not universally semantic requirements, but their relevant projections cannot simply disappear.

## Primary literature: what changes this decision

**READ [Raykov–Schiper–Pedone, OPODIS 2011](https://crypto.ethz.ch/publications/files/RaScPe11.pdf), §§2–5:** generic broadcast orders conflicting commands consistently while permitting commuting delivery orders. Their construction needs n>5f and an atomic-broadcast recovery oracle; speculative replica execution may roll back, but clients receive definitive results. This older work directly matches the independence question better than throughput DAG papers. It is a contract precedent, not a ready Mini implementation or an argument for releasing speculative messages.

**READ [Bailis et al., VLDB/extended paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1402.2237), §4 Definition 6/Theorem 1 and Appendix B:** I-confluence concerns merging all valid reachable states from a common ancestor under a specified merge operator. Commutation alone does not prove invariant preservation. **READ [Kleppmann–Howard, December 2020 v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2012.00472), §§2.2–3.3, §5:** BEC's existence theorem covers I-confluent transactions under its stated replication model, with arbitrary Byzantine replicas, authenticated updates and eventual connectivity among correct replicas. It does not promise linearizable revocation during partitions. Its hash-DAG reconciliation gives useful evidence/deduplication, but predecessor disclosure is not private access-pattern protection.

**READ [Balegas et al., March 31 2015 v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1503.09052), §§II–III, VI:** bounded counters partition numeric rights; local spending consumes owned rights and merge reconciles monotone state. The failure model is crash/recovery with intact persistent memory, not malicious overspending. This is the right narrower primitive for quota fragmentation, with a new Byzantine enforcement obligation for Mini. It does not supply generic independent-object semantics.

**READ [Barbaraci–Cachin, September 16 2026 v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.17074v1), §§3–4:** distinguish acknowledge/schedule/reveal/deliver and model verifiable encapsulation to constrain causal influence through Byzantine parties. This is stronger than trusting self-reported dependencies; its release ordering does not settle unrelated concurrent conflicts. Encapsulated messages ultimately reveal to the protocol's participants, so a private room audience still needs a separate disclosure boundary.

**NEW READ [Patel–Kshemkalyani, September 29 2026 v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.37913v1), §§2–4/Algorithm 1:** four-phase BCRB claims constant wire metadata under f<n/3, authenticated reliable FIFO channels and threshold decryption. It reconstructs barriers through ACK/decryption-share history using an n×n matrix and local vectors. This postdates the earlier survey and Scry's coverage. The result moves dependency bookkeeping rather than removing it; application confidentiality, private conflict tracking, FIFO refinement over CHANNELS and PQ threshold instantiation remain work. Its stronger causality definition differs from the preceding paper; no interchangeability theorem was inspected. Preprint correctness claims are not mechanized or deployment evidence here.

**READ [Albouy et al., QAAT v2, February 19 2025](https://arxiv.org/html/2405.18072v2), §§2–4, 6, Appendix D:** operation-specific asset transfer avoids a global transfer order with single-owner accounts and agreement proofs. Commitments/accumulators/ZK plus an assumed receiver-anonymous channel hide recipient and amount, while sender identity remains exposed. Shared ownership and Mini's general revocable multi-object methods do not inherit that result. This is a concrete comparator for narrowing both the operation contract and disclosure, not evidence that encrypting dependency edges solves generic conflict privacy.

## Exact obligations before construction

1. **Semantic dependency completeness:** derive authenticated reads from every target law/interpreter/library/observation, authority path and charge; prove omitted state cannot alter enabledness, output or effects. Include read/write overlap and replay identity. Do not trust a client-declared footprint.
2. **Mixed-history equivalence:** for reachable legal states, prove classified-independent swaps preserve per-operation observations and invariants; lift this to conflicting plus independent traces. Identify the receipt/history quotient explicitly. Shared height, clock and certification are counterexamples until handled.
3. **Current-authority fence:** define R's completion point and fresh versus historical I; prove no new accepted invocation or confidential output release crosses the fence, including partitions, delayed jobs and policy revisions.
4. **Distributed refinement:** prove quorum intersection/locking, availability and cross-configuration preservation only if BFT controllers are chosen; separately refine live append/readback/crash/retry and per-domain certificate frontiers. Current SystemCell authorization is not that theorem.
5. **Privacy noninterference:** name observer/audience leakage and compare coupled equal-public workloads for conflict labels, predecessor requests, certification and timing. Either price coarse padded domains or add private classification/proofs; the causal papers do not discharge this.
6. **Progress and escrow conservation:** state partition behavior, eventual delivery/service assumptions, antirollback custody, reclaim/transfer uniqueness and bounded-right exhaustion. A safe controller refusing all requests is not an adequate realization.
